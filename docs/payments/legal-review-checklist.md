# PORTAL18 — LEGAL & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE REVIEW CHECKLIST

> **Classification**: Internal Governance / Legal Audit Checklist
> **Status**: PENDING FORMAL LEGAL COUNSEL REVIEW
> **Notice**: This document outlines architectural and contractual items requiring formal validation by accredited Brazilian legal counsel (OAB). It does not constitute definitive legal advice.

---

## 1. Terms of Service & Subscription Agreements
- [ ] **Advertiser Commercial Terms (Termos de Publicidade e Anúncios)**:
  - Clear statement that payment covers platform digital showcase and advertising features only.
  - Explicit disclaimer of employment, agency, or partnership with independent advertisers.
  - Prohibition of unverified third parties, non-consensual media, or illegal solicitation.
- [ ] **Consumer Terms of Use (Termos de Uso do Usuário Visitante)**:
  - Mandatory confirmation of 18+ legal majority and awareness of adult content.
  - Non-transferability of accounts and personal credentials.
- [ ] **Consumer Premium Terms (Assinatura de Membro Premium)**:
  - Specific rights granted by membership (media viewing, review insights).
  - Explicit reminder that Consumer Premium does not guarantee offline availability of third-party advertisers.

---

## 2. Auto-Renewal & Subscription Cancellation Policies
- [ ] **Recurring Subscriptions (Renovação Automática)**:
  - Clear, conspicuous pre-purchase disclosure of billing interval (7, 30, or 90 days) and renewal pricing.
  - Automated transactional confirmation email dispatched upon each cycle renewal.
- [ ] **Cancellation Rights & Grace Period**:
  - Unconditional self-service cancellation mechanism in user/advertiser dashboard.
  - Explicit rule: Entitlement persists until the end of the paid period upon cancellation without retroactive penalty.

---

## 3. Right of Regret & Refund Policy (Código de Defesa do Consumidor - CDC)
- [ ] **Right of Regret (Art. 49 do CDC - Arrependimento de 7 Dias)**:
  - Workflow for full refund if invoked within 7 calendar days of initial digital plan activation.
  - Pro-rata calculation rules for multi-month advance packages (90 days).
- [ ] **Accidental Double Transactions**:
  - Immediate programmatic refund mechanism upon detection of duplicate charge attempts.

---

## 4. Chargebacks, Disputes & Fraud Policies
- [ ] **Chargeback Escalation Protocol**:
  - Documented evidence package (timestamped logs, IP records, KYC confirmation, session IDs) demonstrating proof of digital delivery.
- [ ] **Friendly Fraud & Extortion Protection**:
  - Clear terms stating that abusive or fraudulent dispute filings result in account suspension.

---

## 5. Youth Protection & Criminal Legislation Compliance
- [ ] **ECA Digital (Estatuto da Criança e do Adolescente - Lei 8.069/1990)**:
  - Strict compliance with Arts. 240, 241, 241-A regarding prevention of underage depiction or participation.
  - Audit logs documenting immediate referral to SaferNet, Ministério Público and Polícia Federal upon detection of suspected underage attempts.
- [ ] **Marco Civil da Internet (Lei 12.965/2014)**:
  - Compliance with Art. 21 regarding rapid takedown of intimate or non-consensual imagery upon notification.

---

## 6. Privacy & Data Protection (LGPD - Lei 13.709/2018)
- [ ] **Legal Bases for Processing**:
  - Processing KYC identity data under the legal basis of *Compliance with Legal/Regulatory Obligation* and *Fraud Prevention* (Art. 7, II and X).
  - Processing advertising subscriptions under *Contract Execution* (Art. 7, V).
- [ ] **Data Minimization & Retention Limits**:
  - Automatic purging of ephemeral authenticity video files and expired verification sessions.
  - Segregation of financial transaction logs from personal behavioral data.

---

- [ ] **CNAE Mapping**:
  - CNAE 7312-2/00 — Agenciamento de espaços para publicidade, exceto em veículos de comunicação.
  - CNAE 6319-4/00 — Portais, provedores de conteúdo e outros serviços de informação na internet.
