## 1. Business Model Truth

The Portal18 commercial platform strictly operates as a digital advertising and software-as-a-service (SaaS) provider:

- **What Portal18 Sells**:
  - Digital advertising directory subscriptions for independent advertisers.
  - Consumer Premium utility subscriptions (advanced filters, favorites, privacy controls).
  - Digital visibility boosts (highlight placements in search results).

- **What Portal18 DOES NOT Do**:
  - Does NOT process payments between visitors/clients and advertisers.
  - Does NOT intermediate, split, or escrow payments for private personal services.
  - Does NOT handle client wallets or provide advertiser payouts.
