## 1. Accounting Monetization Model

Portal18 recognizes revenue strictly from:
1. **Advertiser Subscriptions**: Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) and directory presence.
2. **Consumer Premium**: Digital utility subscription for enhanced consumer search tools.
3. **Boost Placements**: Temporary highlight visibility in directory algorithms.

**Excluded Transactions**:
- Zero client-to-advertiser payments.
- Zero escrow or wallet custody.
- Zero payout disbursements.

---

## 2. Statutory Period Close Integrity

Closing a financial period requires zero unresolved P0 blockers:
- Unreconciled payment attempts = 0.
- Unresolved payment chargebacks = 0.
- Unaccounted provider settlement fees = 0.
- Reopening closed periods requires four-eyes authorization and mandatory audit justification.
